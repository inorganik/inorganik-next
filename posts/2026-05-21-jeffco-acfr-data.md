As a dad of 2 JeffCo students (Mt. Carbon elementary and Summit Ridge middle) I have become increasingly curious about our district's budgeting. Our beloved Peiffer Elementary closed in 2023. We occasionally get urgent requests from our daughter's current school to buy supplies they are lacking, and they run 3-5 fundraisers per year. And we continue to see teachers get cut. So I started to take a close look at the data when I heard about yet another budget shortfall.

## [Check out my report here](https://inorganik.net/jeffco-acfr-report)

My hunch was that there has been a growing administration, and shrinking school staff, and unfortunately that appears to be true. In just the last 5 years, teacher count has dropped by 344, but admin personnel has gone up by 49. And in the last 10 years, the general administration category of the budget has more than doubled.

One concerning undercurrent is the declining enrollment, by and large a result of declining birth rates. At a recent school board community forum my wife and I attended, Tina Moeinian cited that 75% of Jefferson county households do not have school aged children, which means passing a mill levy override is becoming increasingly tougher. In my view that means better targeting the budget on schools and their staff.

💻 **On a technological sidenote**: A parent at the forum pointed out that based on a rough cost of $300 per chromebook plus the Lexia and other licenses the district has purchased for the 29K elementary students in JeffCo, the costs are over $10 million. Personally I'd rather see that money go to teachers and the classroom. But based on the assignments my daughter has, JeffCo has become increasingly reliant on digital instructions, with even my daughter's art class involving the laptop.
